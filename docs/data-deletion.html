Account & Data Deletion

Developer: Akash Kumar | App: Echo AI Local

Echo AI Local does not require traditional user accounts (like email/password sign-ups). Instead, it uses an anonymous Node ID generated on your device.

However, if you have synced data to our cloud servers during a disaster and wish to have it permanently removed, you have the right to request deletion. Here are the steps to delete both your local and cloud data.

How to Delete Your Data

1 Delete On-Device Data (Instant)

To delete all offline chat messages, SOS requests, and your local profile from your device:

  • Go to your phone's Settings > Apps > Echo AI Local.
  • Tap Storage & cache.
  • Tap Clear Storage (or Clear Data).
  • Alternatively, you can simply Uninstall the app.
2 Delete Cloud-Synced Data

If your app was connected to the internet, it may have synced distress signals or messages to our AWS cloud server to aid rescue teams. To request deletion of this synced data:

Please send an email to the developer requesting data deletion. If you know your device's Node ID, please include it in the email.

Email Deletion Request

What Data is Deleted / Kept?

  • Data Deleted: Your display name, role, GPS locations, chat messages, and SOS distress requests tied to your Node ID will be permanently purged from our servers.
  • Data Kept: No personal or identifiable data is retained. We only keep anonymized aggregate counts (e.g., total SOS requests made in a region) for statistical purposes.
  • Retention Period: By default, all cloud data is automatically purged after the active disaster relief period concludes. If you request manual deletion, your data will be removed within 7 days of the request.
